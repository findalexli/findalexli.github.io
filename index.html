About

I am a Research Engineer at Meta, working on post-training for the Llama model family. My research focuses on Large Language Models, Multi-modal LLMs, and Alignment. I am passionate about developing efficient and accessible AI systems that benefit society while ensuring safety and reliability.

Previously, I was a Member of Technical Staff at Reka AI (contributing to Reka-flash-3), Staff LLM Data Scientist at TIFIN (a JPMorgan-backed fintech), and Data Scientist at Cala Health, where I co-invented three patents on wearable neurostimulation devices now covered by Medicare.
