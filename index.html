Shengzhi (Alex) Li

I am a Research Engineer at Meta, working on Muse post-training at Meta Superintelligence Labs. I have contributed to post-training of two model families: Muse at Meta and the Reka Flash series (Reka Flash 3 / 3.1) at Reka AI. Recently I co-led SWE-Together, a benchmark that evaluates coding agents in interactive, multi-turn sessions with real users, and released EdgeBench Verified, an audit of the hidden graders behind the EdgeBench agent leaderboard. Before that, I was at Reka AI, TIFIN (JPMorgan-backed fintech), and Cala Health (3 patents on neurostimulation devices, now Medicare-covered). My research focuses on LLM post-training and evaluation, coding agents, AI for scientific research, and multi-modal LLMs.

EdgeBench Verified: An Audit of the Graders Behind the 51 Public EdgeBench Tasks
Shengzhi Li
Technical report, Oct 2026
Tests each hidden EdgeBench grader with correct, broken and to-the-letter submissions. Graders in 32 of 51 tasks score some submissions wrongly (e.g. correct solutions scored zero, broken programs given full credit, one task capped at 21/100); fixes published for 31 tasks and verified side by side. The top two leaderboard places can no longer be confirmed.
[Website] [中文] [Code & Fixes]
SWE-Together: Evaluating Coding Agents in Interactive User Sessions
Yifan Wu*, Zhuokai Zhao, Songlin Li, Ho Hin Lee, Jiacheng Zhu, Shirley Wu, Tianhe Yu, Serena Li, Lizhu Zhang, Xiangjun Fan, Shengzhi Li*
ICLR 2026 Workshop · arXiv 2026 (cited 18 times) · *Project Co-Lead
A multi-turn coding-agent benchmark of 109 repository-level tasks reconstructed from 11,260 real user–agent sessions, replayed with a reactive user simulator. Agents are scored on both final correctness and how many user corrections they need.

AppliedScientist: Automated Scientific Revision Through Iterative AI Reviewing
Vidushee Vats, Karun Sharma, Shengzhi Li, Shichao Pei
arXiv 2026
A closed-loop system pairing an autonomous AI scientist with an AI reviewer to revise rejected ICLR papers. Reviewer-guided revision raises scores from 5.37 to 6.53 and resolves 85% of execution-related weaknesses, but only 11% of idea-related ones.

Haowen Guan, Shengzhi Li, Shichao Pei

Uses a cheap low-resolution video preview to decide where to spend a limited high-resolution frame budget, trained with privileged self-distillation from an answer-aware teacher.
